Dell Technologies Rewards Landing Page

I designed a conversion-focused landing page experience that simplified Dell Rewards messaging, improved scanability, and guided users toward enrollment through stronger hierarchy and structure.

24% Enrollment Start Rate
19% Lower-Than-Benchmark Bounce Rate
17% CTA Engagement Rate
Objective

Translate a feature-rich rewards program into a simple, compelling story while making enrollment the clearest next action.

Role

UI/Visual Designer
Acquisition landing page design

Team
Me PM DV
Timeframe

3 weeks

Overview

Dell Rewards included multiple promotional offers, financing details, and membership benefits, creating the challenge of presenting a large amount of information without overwhelming users. The experience needed to communicate value quickly while guiding users through a clear and structured enrollment journey across desktop and mobile.

Design Objectives

The challenge was designing a rewards-focused acquisition experience that balanced promotional messaging, financing information, and membership benefits without creating cognitive overload. The structure needed to help users understand the value quickly while keeping enrollment actions clear throughout the experience.

Who it's for

  • Users comparing membership and rewards value quickly
  • Shoppers deciding whether to enroll before purchasing
  • Visitors who need confidence in how points and benefits work
  • Users on desktop and mobile who expect a frictionless sign-up path

What the experience needed to accomplish

  • Communicate rewards and financing clearly above the fold
  • Create a structured enrollment journey across the page
  • Balance promotional messaging without overwhelming users
  • Make benefits easier to scan quickly
  • Maintain hierarchy across desktop and mobile layouts
  • Reinforce trust through product-forward design
  • Create consistent enrollment actions throughout the experience

Wireframing & Layout Structure

Because this was a from-scratch landing page, low-fidelity wireframes were used to establish page structure before moving into visual design. The goal was to plan hierarchy, section pacing, and the enrollment path first, then bring the experience to life with Dell's brand system and product imagery.

Rather than showing the full landing page wireframe as one long image, the process is shown section by section. Each view compares the early layout structure with a hi-fidelity placeholder so the strategic decisions are easier to review.

The hero section was structured to communicate Dell Rewards value immediately while keeping the enrollment path clear and visually dominant.

Page Flow

The experience was intentionally structured to guide users from understanding the rewards program to enrollment without overwhelming them with information too early. Each section was designed to answer a specific user question in sequence: what the program offers, how rewards work, why membership is valuable, and how to enroll.

Flow Breakdown

  • How Dell Rewards Works: Step-by-step clarity on joining, earning, and redeeming.
  • Earning Points: Visual explanation of how purchases build value.
  • Exclusive Offers: Benefits framed as member-only advantages.
  • FAQ: Objection handling to reduce enrollment hesitation.

Design Approach

The visual system focused on hierarchy, spacing, and modular content structure to make rewards information easier to scan while maintaining consistency with Dell's existing brand language.

The layout used Dell's signature blue palette, structured spacing, and product-forward visuals to create a clean, trustworthy experience that guided attention naturally toward enrollment actions.

Execution

The execution focused on building a structured acquisition page from the ground up, using clear hierarchy, modular content, and consistent CTA placement to support the enrollment journey.

How the experience was structured

  • Prioritized the rewards value proposition above the fold
  • Used modular content sections to improve scanability
  • Designed clear CTA hierarchy throughout the experience
  • Simplified large blocks of information into digestible sections
  • Used product imagery to reinforce trust and legitimacy
  • Built responsive layouts that maintained hierarchy across screen sizes
  • Created consistent spacing and pacing between sections
  • Structured the page to reduce cognitive overload

Wireframe → Final Design

Because this was a net-new landing page, there was no existing experience to compare against. Instead, this view shows how the page evolved from a low-fidelity wireframe into a polished, conversion-focused Dell Rewards landing page.

The wireframe focused on structure, section order, CTA placement, and content hierarchy. The final design translated that framework into a branded experience with stronger visual hierarchy, product-forward imagery, clearer rewards messaging, and a more refined enrollment path.

Dell Rewards Launch Performance

After launch, the Dell Rewards landing page showed strong engagement across key acquisition metrics. The page structure helped users understand the rewards value quickly, stay engaged through the content, and move toward enrollment through consistent CTA placement across desktop and mobile.

Learnings

This project reinforced how important hierarchy and pacing are when designing acquisition-focused landing pages with large amounts of promotional and rewards content. The challenge was not adding more information, but organizing the experience in a way that helped users understand the value quickly without feeling overwhelmed.

It also reinforced the importance of designing modular systems that maintain clarity across desktop and mobile experiences. Many of the strongest decisions came from simplifying layout structure, creating more intentional section flow, and guiding attention toward a single clear enrollment path.
